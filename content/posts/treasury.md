### Stories:

Story 1 — BMW
Treasury Controls, Accuracy & Process Improvement

At BMW China Leasing, I supported finance operations involving more than 20,000 monthly vehicle payment transactions.

During one reporting cycle, I identified discrepancies between transaction records and management reports. Given the importance of reporting accuracy, I used SQL and Excel to trace the data, investigate the root cause, and validate the information across different datasets.

After identifying the issue, I worked with relevant teams to improve the reporting logic and introduced additional validation checks to strengthen reporting controls.

As a result, reporting accuracy improved and the risk of similar issues occurring in the future was reduced.

This experience taught me the importance of accuracy, reconciliation, operational controls, and continuous process improvement when working with financial data.

Treasury控制、准确性与流程优化

在确认问题后，我与相关团队合作优化了报表逻辑，并增加了额外的数据验证环节，以加强报表控制流程。

最终报表准确率得到提升，同时降低了未来出现类似问题的风险。

这段经历让我深刻理解了准确性、对账、内部控制以及持续流程优化的重要性，而这些也是Treasury工作的核心内容。

*** 15. What is your understanding of Treasury? ***

From my understanding, Treasury is responsible for ensuring that a company has the financial resources, controls, and processes needed to support both its day-to-day operations and long-term growth.

At a high level, Treasury typically covers four key areas.

The first is Cash Management, which focuses on understanding where the company's cash is, how much is available, and ensuring funds are available when and where they are needed.

The second is Payments Management, which ensures that transactions are executed accurately, securely, and on time while maintaining appropriate controls and approvals.

The third is Liquidity Management, which is the area I find particularly interesting. Treasury teams need to understand future cash inflows and outflows, forecast funding requirements, and ensure that the business has sufficient liquidity to meet its obligations. A company can be profitable on paper, but if cash is not available at the right time, it can still face operational challenges. For a global company like JD, where funds are spread across different countries, currencies, and banking relationships, effective liquidity management becomes even more critical.

The fourth is Risk Management, which involves identifying and managing financial risks such as foreign exchange risk, interest rate risk, and liquidity risk.

However, after speaking with Angie and learning more about the team, I realised that Treasury is much more than managing cash and payments. It is also about building reliable processes, maintaining strong controls, supporting business decisions, and continuously improving operational efficiency.

What particularly attracts me to Treasury is that it combines finance, operations, risk management, data analysis, and problem-solving. It requires attention to detail, sound judgement, strong communication skills, and the ability to identify issues while also proposing practical solutions.

I also appreciate that Treasury is a function where continuous learning and ownership are very important. Initially, the focus is on understanding processes and controls, but over time, you are expected to develop your own understanding of the business, identify opportunities for improvement, and become capable of independently analysing information and making recommendations.

That combination of operational responsibility, analytical thinking, and continuous improvement is one of the main reasons why I see Treasury as a long-term career path.

在我看来，Treasury的核心职责是确保企业拥有支持日常运营和长期发展的资金、控制体系以及管理流程。

从职能角度来看，Treasury主要包括四个方面。

第一是现金管理（Cash Management），确保企业清楚了解资金状况，并在正确的时间和地点拥有足够的现金支持业务运营。

第二是支付管理（Payments Management），确保各类付款准确、安全、及时地执行，并符合相应的审批和控制要求。

第三是流动性管理（Liquidity Management），这也是我个人特别感兴趣的部分。Treasury团队需要持续预测未来的资金流入和流出情况，提前规划资金需求，并确保企业始终拥有足够的流动性支持业务发展。一家公司即使账面盈利，如果在关键时点缺少现金，依然可能面临运营风险。对于像JD这样拥有全球业务布局的企业来说，资金分布在不同国家、不同币种以及不同银行体系中，因此如何高效调配资金、保障流动性就显得尤为重要。

第四是风险管理（Risk Management），包括汇率风险、利率风险以及流动性风险等财务风险的识别和管理。

不过，通过和Angie的交流，我发现Treasury不仅仅是管理资金和付款流程。它本质上也是一个通过流程、控制和数据支持企业运营和商业决策的重要职能。

我特别认同团队提到的一些要求，例如准确性优先于速度、具备清晰判断能力、能够主动分析问题并提出解决方案，而不仅仅是发现问题。我觉得这些能力其实与优秀Treasury人员的核心素质高度一致。

同时，我认为Treasury也是一个需要持续学习和不断成长的岗位。从最初理解流程和控制体系，到逐渐形成自己的业务理解，再到主动发现优化机会、独立完成分析和报告，这种成长路径让我觉得非常有吸引力。

正是这种结合了财务、运营、风险管理、数据分析以及持续改进的特点，让我认为Treasury是一个非常适合我长期发展的职业方向。
