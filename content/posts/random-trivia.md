1.The name “Bluetooth” actually comes from a 10th-century Danish king.

Harald Bluetooth helped unite Denmark and promote Christianity across the kingdom.

Modern Bluetooth technology was named after him because it was designed to “unite” different devices through wireless connection — just as Harald united parts of Scandinavia.
